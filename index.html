Every Standard Die

d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100. Mix up to 10 dice at once. Click any die on the tray to remove it. Tap a preset and you're rolling in one click.

⚡
Shared Tables — Live

Create a table, share the code, and every player's rolls appear instantly in a shared feed. No accounts needed to join. Works across any device, any location.

🔐
Cryptographically Fair

TTDice uses your browser's crypto.getRandomValues — the same API used for TLS encryption. No biased Math.random(). Your dice are as fair as your browser can make them.

⚔
Quick Presets

Attack roll, Fireball (8d6), Stat roll (4d6), Sneak Attack — one tap to load the right dice for the moment without counting and clicking.

📊
Advantage & Disadvantage

Add two identical dice to unlock Advantage/Disadvantage mode. The app rolls both, shows you both results, and automatically picks the higher or lower — exactly like the rules say.

📜
History That Persists

Your roll history and session stats survive page refreshes. Sign in and they follow you across every device. Never lose track of that legendary nat 20 moment.

✨
Natural 20 Moments

Roll a natural 20 and the tray knows. Gold flash, big energy. Roll a 1 and the table feels it. Because those moments matter and they should look like they matter.

Desktop at home, phone at the game store, tablet on the couch — TTDice is fully responsive and works on any modern browser with no installation required.
